Presented during the 18th Annual Get It D.O.N.E. Texas Promotores Association Conference at South Padre Island, these short videos introduce the FloDisMod initiative and its vector-borne disease awareness campaign.

<!-- more -->

In this impactful video series, we introduce the FloDisMod initiative and take the First Step Against Vectors in our vector-borne diseases awareness campaign. The videos educate viewers on diseases like Dengue, Zika, and Malaria transmitted by mosquitoes, ticks, and fleas, and promote key protective actions:

- Use repellent
- Wear protective clothing
- Eliminate standing water

The videos are available in both English and Spanish.

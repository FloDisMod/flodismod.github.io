The University of Texas Campaign Impact created a video, "Solutions of the Future Start Now". This video describes ongoing computational research that tackles "tough" problems, from natural disasters to cancer, and features work from Professor Clint Dawson, the scientific lead of FloDisMod. In the video Professor Dawson points out that the computational tools developed for understanding storm surge, originally as a research project, are now being used by agencies to make decisions that save lives and property and ultimately, protect the public.

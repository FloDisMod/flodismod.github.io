Stone Nwamadi, a public health major at the University of Texas at Austin has wins an Empowering Future Agricultural Scientists' scholarship with the University of Texas Rio Grande Valley., 

<!-- more -->

The Empowering Future Agricultural Scientists (EFAS) is a program based at the University of Texas Rio Grande Valley headed by Dr. Patricia Feria. It provides scholarships for undergraduate college students in Texas to gain hands-on experimental learning in projects in the agricultural sector. Mr. Stone Nwamadi was awarded a scholarship for 2024-2025 to pursue research aimed at describing, using geospatial tools, the vulnerability of agricultural lands in the lower Rio Grande Valley to flooding. 

A detailed description of his experience can be found at [here](https://www.utrgv.edu/efas/projects/students/stone-nwamadi/index.htm#:~:text=I%20have%20had%20a%20great,software%2C%20pressing%20environmental%20issues%2C%20and).

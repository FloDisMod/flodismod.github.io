Members of FloDIsMod team join the inaugural Open Source Program Office User Advisory Group at the University of Texas at Austin.

<!-- more -->
Alex Marsden, Michael Shensky and Katherine Brown from the FloDisMod team have joined the inaugural User Advisory Group for the Open Source Program Office (OSPO) at the University of Texas at Austin. The OSPO  is the center of open source activity and enables open source practices . The OSPO receives support from the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation (G-2023-20944). It is a central hub for open source support empowering researchers to leverage training, events, and other resources in advancing the open source ecosystem.  

You can read more [here](https://opensource.utexas.edu/).

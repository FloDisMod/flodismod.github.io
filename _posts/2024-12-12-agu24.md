Dr. Katherine Brown presented an invited talk,  "Integrated Models of Flooding and Pathogen Distribution in Coastal Habitats" in the Ocean Sciences program at the American Geophysical Union annual meeting (AGU24), December 9-13 in Washington D.C.. This talk was part of a larger program, "Advances in Flood Prediction and Risk Assessment in Coastal, Inland and Transition Zones". Dr. Al Cerrone was also a convener and chair for this program. 

You can read more [here](https://agu.confex.com/agu/agu24/meetingapp.cgi/Paper/1758789).

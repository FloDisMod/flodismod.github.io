As part of our accessibility efforts, our team is currently developing a 3D-printed outreach keychain that integrates Braille text that links to the upcoming FloDisMod website.

<!-- more -->

Final versions will include multi-language support (English & Spanish), a flood/disease icon, or community-informed design elements. This initiative reflects our commitment to universal design and STEM outreach in the RGV and beyond.

The Subtropical Agriculture and Environments Society Meeting was held in Mission, Texas this year and included a number of presentations from the FloDisMod Team. Dr. Paty Feria spoke about the importance of mentoring in the EFAS program. Dr. Katherine Brown presented a talk on integration of flood and disease models. Five posters were presented from the team by Alvaro Zavala, Rebeca Santos, Stone Nwamadi, Marissa Llamas and Ira Narang.

Alvaro and Rebeca presented during the Subtropical Agriculture and Environments Society Meeting about FloDisMod, the objectives of the project, methods, and conclusions. During the meeting we had the opportunity to receive feedback and ideas about how to continue improving and measure our impact on the community.


You can read more [here](https://www.subplantsci.org/2025/02/).

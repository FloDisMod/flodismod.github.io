Marissa Llamas, a mathematics major at the University of Texas at Austin has won an Empowering Future Agricultural Scientists’ scholarship with the University of Texas Rio Grande Valley. 

<!-- more -->

The Empowering Future Agricultural Scientists (EFAS) is a program based at the University of Texas Rio Grande Valley headed by Dr. Patricia Feria. It provides scholarships for undergraduate college students in Texas to gain hands-on experimental learning in projects in the agricultural sector. Ms. Marissa Llamas was awarded a scholarship for 2024-2025 to pursue research aimed at modelling flooding in the Lower Rio Grande Valley. 

A detailed description of her experience can be found at [here](https://www.utrgv.edu/efas/projects/students/marissa-llamas/index.htm).

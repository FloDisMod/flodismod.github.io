His research interests lie at the intersection of computational modelling and large-scale physical phenomena like storm surge and disease transmission.  He currently operates NOAA's development instance of STOFS-2D-Global at the University of Notre Dame, and he works to integrate FloDisMod's environmental niche models within it.
